# Home

Welcome to the **Exercise Physiology Laboratory (LaFiSE)**.

The Exercise Physiology Laboratory is dedicated to investigating the physiological mechanisms underlying physical exercise using **animal models**. Our research integrates physiology, neurophysiology, and behavioral neuroscience to better understand how exercise influences the nervous system and overall organism function.

Our research focuses on five main areas:

- 💤 Sleep Physiology
- 🧠 Learning and Memory
- 🐁 Behavioral Neuroscience
- ⚡ Electrophysiology
- 🔬 Open Science and Reproducible Research

---

## About the Laboratory

The laboratory is coordinated by **Prof. dr. Hindiael Belchior** and is committed to conducting high-quality, reproducible scientific research while training undergraduate and graduate students in experimental physiology and neuroscience.

We investigate how different exercise protocols influence neural activity, sleep, cognition, and behavior through multidisciplinary experimental approaches.
